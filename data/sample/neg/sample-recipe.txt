Heat the oven to four hundred degrees. Mix the flour, sugar, and butter in a large bowl until the mixture looks like coarse crumbs. Add the eggs one at a time, stirring gently, then fold in the chocolate chips. Spread the batter evenly in a lined baking pan. Bake for twenty five minutes, or until a skewer inserted in the centre comes out clean. Let the cake cool in the pan for ten minutes before turning it out onto a wire rack.
